- 日期：2026-11-16
- 作者：未提供
- 文章类型：Journal Article
- 研究问题：Precise gene expression control is fundamental for biological research and synthetic systems.
- 核心发现：
  1. While biophysical models predict transcriptional or translational regulation, they often overlook their potential interplay.
  1. Combining these regulations offers synergistic control, but its influence remains incompletely understood.
  1. RESULTS: Here, we investigated how promoter replacement affects 5' untranslated region (5' UTR)-mediated translation while designing the sequence of Escherichia coli.
- 摘要：MOTIVATION: Precise gene expression control is fundamental for biological research and synthetic systems. While biophysical models predict transcriptional or translational regulation, they often overlook their potential interplay. Combining these regulations offers synergistic control, but its influence remains incompletely understood.

RESULTS: Here, we investigated how promoter replacement affects 5' untranslated region (5' UTR)-mediated translation while designing the sequence of Escherichia coli. Using predictive models and a comprehensive library of promoter-5' UTR pairs, we characterized the interference of promoters with translation rates in terms of mRNA secondary structures, especially the alternative folding of the Shine-Dalgarno (SD) region and frequency of the major transcription start sites (TSSs), which leads to the misprediction of gene expression.

AVAILABILITY: Based on this, we developed UTR Designer Pro, a model for designing promoter-5' UTR sequences with higher accuracy, now available as a web service at https://sbi.postech.ac.kr/utr_designer_pro/. Our work improves understanding of transcriptional and translational regulation, aiding gene expression control in bacterial systems.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Supplementary data are available at Bioinformatics online.
