摘要：准确的分子特性预测（MPP）仍然是药物发现的核心挑战。鉴于标记分子数据的可用性有限，多模式分子表示学习为整合 MPP 的互补结构信息提供了一种有前途的方法。现有的多模态模型仍然面临两个局限性：（i）常见的融合策略，特别是直接串联，可能会引入与目标属性弱相关的冗余信息或信号；（ii）将互补的物理化学先验有效纳入多模态表示的探索尚未充分。结果在这里，我们提出了 ReMoD-Mol，一种集成了分子图、SMILES 序列和二维分子图像的可靠性感知多模态框架。任务自适应模态校准从下游监督中学习了每个基准的一组权重，以调节三种分子视图的相对贡献。基于描述符和指纹的教师通过仅训练的五重折叠知识蒸馏进一步提供了任务条件化学先验。原型引导的嵌入成形与图视图和 SMILES 视图一致性目标一起规范了融合的表示空间。在涉及回归和分类的 9 个 MoleculeNet 基准数据集上，ReMoD-Mol 在 7 个数据集上实现了最佳平均性能，在与 14 个基线进行支架分割比较时，在另外两个数据集上实现了第二好。表示分析揭示了更平滑的属性相关流形和更具辨别力的类结构，而学习的模态系数则表现出明显的任务相关融合模式。 教师先验和消融分析进一步支持化学先验迁移和多视图表示正则化的互补作用。可用性和实施​​源代码、处理的数据集和实施细节可在 https://github.com/27167199/ReMoD-Mol 上公开获取

  • 作者：未提供
  • 文章类型：Journal Article
  • 研究问题：Precise gene expression control is fundamental for biological research and synthetic systems.

核心发现：

  1. While biophysical models predict transcriptional or translational regulation, they often overlook their potential interplay.
  2. Combining these regulations offers synergistic control, but its influence remains incompletely understood.
  3. RESULTS: Here, we investigated how promoter replacement affects 5' untranslated region (5' UTR)-mediated translation while designing the sequence of Escherichia coli.

摘要：MOTIVATION: Precise gene expression control is fundamental for biological research and synthetic systems. While biophysical models predict transcriptional or translational regulation, they often overlook their potential interplay. Combining these regulations offers synergistic control, but its influence remains incompletely understood. RESULTS: Here, we investigated how promoter replacement affects 5' untranslated region (5' UTR)-mediated translation while designing the sequence of Escherichia coli. Using predictive models and a comprehensive library of promoter-5' UTR pairs, we characterized the interference of promoters with translation rates in terms of mRNA secondary structures, especially the alternative folding of the Shine-Dalgarno (SD) region and frequency of the major transcription start sites (TSSs), which leads to the misprediction of gene expression. AVAILABILITY: Based on this, we developed UTR Designer Pro, a model for designing promoter-5' UTR sequences with higher accuracy, now available as a web service at https://sbi.postech.ac.kr/utr_designer_pro/. Our work improves understanding of transcriptional and translational regulation, aiding gene expression control in bacterial systems. SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Supplementary data are available at Bioinformatics online.
