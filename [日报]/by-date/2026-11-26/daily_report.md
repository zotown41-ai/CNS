#### 针对FFPE组织样本进行高效多重成像分析，需要对组织制备过程及染色条件进行大规模定量评估。 / Large-scale quantitative assessment of tissue preparation and staining conditions for robust multiplexed imaging of FFPE tissue samples
- Nature methods · 2026-11-26 · 有摘要

- 论文摘要（出版社摘要 · 中文译文）：多重蛋白质成像技术或空间蛋白组学方法能够以单细胞分辨率同时检测完整组织中的50多种蛋白质，从而帮助人们深入了解组织的结构特征及细胞间的相互作用。然而，不同的组织制备与染色流程往往会导致实验结果出现差异，这严重限制了不同研究及不同技术平台之间的可比性。因此，我们开展了一项大规模、多地点、多平台的基准测试研究，专门针对经福尔马林固定、石蜡包埋处理后的组织样本，系统评估了24种不同的抗原修复与抗体染色组合方案。通过使用包含27种标志物的免疫检测体系，并结合能够反映信号强度、实验结果的稳定性以及标志物检测效果的各种定量指标，我们对这些方案的性能进行了全面评价。结果表明，以Tris-EDTA为基础的抗原修复方法与在4℃下进行过夜抗体染色相结合的使用方案始终表现出最高的检测效率，而几种常用的替代方案则存在明显的性能缺陷。通过在多个学术机构及工业实验室中采用不同的成像技术对实验结果进行验证，我们进一步确认了这些结论的普遍适用性。此外，我们还发现标志物的信号强度与组织中的空间异质性呈正相关关系，这一现象有助于更准确地检测各种不同类型的细胞群体。综上所述，我们的研究结果为提高空间蛋白组学研究的重复性、规范实验操作流程以及促进跨研究间的数据比较提供了实用的指导原则与标准化评估方法。
- 英文原文：Multiplexed protein imaging or spatial proteomics enables the simultaneous measurement of over 50 proteins in intact tissues at single-cell resolution, providing insights into tissue organization and cellular interactions. However, variation in tissue preparation and staining protocols remains a major source of technical variability, limiting reproducibility and comparability across studies and platforms. Here we present a large-scale, multisite and multi-platform benchmarking study of tissue preparation conditions for formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tissues. We systematically evaluated 24 combinations of antigen retrieval and antibody staining conditions using a 27-marker immune panel and ranked performance using quantitative metrics that capture signal intensity, variability and marker failure. Tris–EDTA-based antigen retrieval combined with overnight antibody staining at 4 °C consistently achieved the highest performance, whereas several commonly used alternatives showed reduced robustness. Validation across additional academic and industry laboratories and multiple imaging technologies confirmed the generalizability of these findings across tissues and platforms. We further show that marker signal intensity correlated with greater spatial heterogeneity, improving the detection of diverse cell populations. Together, our results provide practical guidelines and a standardized benchmarking strategy to improve reproducibility, harmonize workflows and facilitate cross-study comparisons in spatial proteomics.
